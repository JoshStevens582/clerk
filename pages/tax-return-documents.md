# Tax return documents we need

When we prepare your personal tax return, please provide:

- Your IRP5 or IT3(a) from each employer for the tax year
- Bank statements for interest earned (all accounts)
- Medical aid tax certificate (if applicable)
- Retirement annuity contribution certificates
- Proof of donations to registered public benefit organisations
- Rental income schedule if you let property
- Travel logbook if you claim a travel allowance

We do not start work until we have these items or a written list of what is still outstanding.
